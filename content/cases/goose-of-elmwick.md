# The Goose of Elmwick (spoilers)

Curious theft, absurd. Files: `goose-of-elmwick.en.json`, `goose-of-elmwick.ru.json`.

## Solution

Wilhelmina Tuck (`postmaster`), the retired postmaster, took the stuffed goose Admiral Quill at minute 130 (ten past two) from `museum-hall`. Her late husband's love letters were hidden in the goose's stand, and the charity auction was about to sell them. She opened the service door with an old spare staff card, had the apprentice hold a handcart for a pound of toffee, and carried the goose out in a hatbox. It is under the stamp counter at the post office.

Motive deduction: `motive-love-letters` (`stand-hollow` + `letters-rumour`).

## Evidence chains

Three independent chains, each enough to name the culprit, each on different skills:

| Chain | Clues                                                       | Skills                                                         |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A     | `feather-trail`, `ink-smudge` (archive), `stand-hollow`     | perception (the archive needs `archive-key`, found by looking) |
| B     | `hatbox-witness` (tea shop), `letters-rumour` (post office) | social, contacts                                               |
| C     | `door-log`, `apprentice-confession` (boiler room)           | tech, pressure                                                 |

`sale-catalogue` (reasoning) explains why the stand mattered and sits behind the curator's office, a social check of 11. It is not in a chain.

## Red herrings

- `ransom-note` and `wet-boots` point at the caterer Quentin Moss. The note is a prank and the crumbs are from his burnt biscuits.
- `gold-cufflink` points at the curator Alaric Pell, who merely sleeps in his office before galas.

## Lies

Each suspect has one lie broken by clues, for example the postmaster's parcel alibi is broken by `door-log` and `hatbox-witness`.

## Routes by hero

- Former auditor (reasoning, perception): chain A.
- Street private eye (social, contacts): chain B, plus the curator's office.
- Investigative blogger (tech, pressure): chain C.

Scenes: the courtyard is a `crowd`, the boiler room is a `confined-space`, `door-log` is `digital-evidence`. No clue depends on police data, so no trait locks a route out. All difficulties are at most 12.
