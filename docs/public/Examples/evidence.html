The Fee That Was Never Filed

Turn 50 · Day 2 of 3, 14:35 · lost · Detective: Quinn Tindall

Working time left: 25 h 25 min · Condition: nerves frayed, rested, ill-equipped, name in doubt

In Tosora a court has fined the editor Ilvane Marrick for running a news website without a publication licence and sealed her newsroom. She swears she paid the licence fee. The Office of Publication Licences has no record of it and, asked where a licence is actually obtained, gives three different answers. Quinn Tindall is sent to find where the money went.
